Definitions

  • The whole page uses the median, not the mean (a mean is pulled by one extreme price)
  • "Comp-set median" = the middle value of the hotels with a normal price that day; the count is shown when it is short, and "—" appears when fewer than 2 hotels have a price
  • The number of hotels that can be compared changes every day, so this column read down the page is not a trend
  • Every percentage is one number on the page divided by another, so you can check it
  • Lowest bookable price under the same search conditions every run (1 room, 1 night, 1 adult); dates roll forward

Price cells

  • Normal prices have no background; the small figure below = change from the previous run, + higher, − lower, blank if unchanged
  • A figure with a grey dotted underline = only a suite or other high-price room type was left (above 2.5 times that hotel’s own median, applied once there are at least 10 normal days); excluded from the median and the chart
  • Sold out = no rooms left, with the previous run’s price below; No price = nothing was read. Neither is ever filled in
  • A "N sold out" tag next to a date = two or more hotels sold out that day

Hotels

  • You = Harbour View Hotel; Central Park Hotel = Central Park Hotel; Marina Bay Suites = Marina Bay Suites; Old Town Inn = Old Town Inn; Seaside Resort = Seaside Resort
  • Add or remove a hotel and every percentage is recalculated
